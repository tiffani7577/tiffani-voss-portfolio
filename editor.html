How to use the Editor

✏️Edit Text: Click any text on the site preview. It will highlight in pink. Edit it in the left panel — changes show live.
☰Sections: Switch to the Sections tab to drag and reorder sections, or hide ones you don't want shown.
💾Save Changes: Click "Save Changes" to lock in your edits. Then click "Download HTML" to get the updated file.
📱Device Preview: Use the buttons at the top of the preview to see how the site looks on desktop, tablet, or mobile.
⚠️This editor works best when opened from the same folder as index.html. Open editor.html in Chrome.
